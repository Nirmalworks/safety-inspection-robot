Before fine-tuning each U-Net fails one mark: the generative one erases too much, the erase-only one too little. Fine-tuned on lab maps both pass, and the erase-only U-Net removes more clutter and cannot draw a wall. Every fine-tuned model localises within 9 cm on the recorded drive. STANDARD METRICS, IF ASKED. Our two marks are the recall of each class: structure kept is recall of the keep class, clutter removed is recall of the erase class.
