The best map is that fine-tuned erase-only U-Net combined with session agreement: the U-Net decides the cells the other maps do not agree on. It is safer for the walls: 98.7 percent kept. The red cells are wall taken from other days’ maps, not invented. For the patrol, the planned tour is 5 metres shorter and localisation on a replayed drive is unchanged. The limit: in a building it never saw it removes a third of the clutter. The limits are collected on the last slide of the deck. Next step: fine-tune on a few hand-labelled maps of a second building and test there, to see whether a handful of labelled maps does for a new place what it did for our lab.
