What these results do not show

One room

Both marks are passed in our lab only. In a building no model saw, a third of the clutter is removed and F1 falls from 82.6 % to 44.3 %.

Hand labels

Scores rest on 4 maps labelled by hand, by one person, starting from session-agreement proposals. Fine-tuning needs one hand-labelled map of each new room.

Few training runs

The fine-tuned erase-only U-Net was trained three times: 3.5 points of spread in clutter removed. Differences of a few points between models are not established.

The best map

It needs other mapping sessions of the same room, and its score depends on which hand labels exist: 72.8 % or 69.0 % of clutter removed.

Unseen-building labels

MIT Stata labels come from floor plans without furniture, so fixed furniture counts as clutter. Every score there is lowered by an unknown amount.
