About 60 seconds for this slide and the eight that follow. They are one slide in nine steps: each step shows the next method in the table on the same test map, from a day no model trained on, and highlights its row. This first one is the raw map as recorded. Every method cleaned the same four hand-labelled lab maps. MOVED OFF THE SLIDE: the pass marks are at least 95 percent of structure kept and at least 60 percent of clutter removed, shown green when passed and red when not; the figures are the mean over 4 hand-labelled lab maps, each from a day the model never saw; clutter is the positive class, so TP is clutter erased, FN clutter left, TN structure kept, FP structure erased. THE FORMULA TABLE: with clutter as the positive class, structure kept is the recall of the structure class (specificity) and clutter removed is the recall of the clutter class. On this slide both are counted with a small distance tolerance: a structure cell counts as kept if a wall remains within 10 cm, a clutter cell as removed only if nothing is left within 5 cm. Precision, F1 and accuracy are counted on the exact cell, without that tolerance.
