AME 505 · Group 5 · Autonomous Safety Inspection Robot · final presentation

About 60 seconds. Eight clicks step the picture on the right through the methods in table order, on one test map from a day no model trained on; say each line as its picture appears. Every method cleaned the same four hand-labelled lab maps. The baselines keep the walls and leave the clutter: a speckle filter removes 10 percent, the published ROSE2 method 26, comparing sessions 53. A generative U-Net, which outputs the whole map again, trained only on simulated maps removes 86 percent of the clutter and keeps 87.6 percent of the structure, below the mark. Fine-tuned on lab maps it passes both marks. The erase-only U-Net trained only on simulated maps keeps the walls, 97.1 percent, but removes only half the clutter, because its threshold is unstable from map to map. Fine-tuned on lab maps it passes both marks: 96.6 percent kept, 74 removed, on maps from a day it never saw, and by construction it cannot draw a wall. The best map is that fine-tuned erase-only U-Net combined with session agreement: the U-Net decides the cells the other maps do not agree on. It is safer for the walls: 98.7 percent kept. For the patrol, the planned tour is 5 metres shorter and localisation on a replayed drive is unchanged. The limit: in a building it never saw it removes a third of the clutter. Next step: fine-tune on a few hand-labelled maps of a second building and test there, to see whether a handful of labelled maps does for a new place what it did for our lab. DETAILS MOVED OFF THE SLIDE: the erase-only U-Net over 3 seeds gives 96.5 to 97.0 percent and 71.0 to 74.5; the simulated-only erase threshold is set on a lab map of another day, and that model removes 69.6 percent on the pictured map against 51.3 on average because its threshold is unstable; the best map is the fine-tuned erase-only U-Net with session agreement from other days’ maps, then the door leak cut and unseen gaps filled, built without the test map’s own labels; the pictures are one test map from 25 Nov, every method after the same clean-up, and the numbers are the 4-map mean. IF ASKED: three training runs give 96.5 to 97 percent kept and 71 to 74.5 removed.
